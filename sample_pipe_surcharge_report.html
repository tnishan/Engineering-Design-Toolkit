6. Notes and limitations

Boussinesq gives the FREE-FIELD vertical stress in undisturbed ground - it does not model the pipe. A rigid pipe stiffer than its surround attracts more load than this; a flexible pipe sheds load into the sidefill and feels less. Converting this to a design pressure needs a bedding factor and a pipe-stiffness assessment.
The half-space is assumed homogeneous, isotropic and linearly elastic. Compacted granular backfill over softer native soil does not behave that way, and any rigid pavement or running surface above will spread load far more than this predicts.
The load-spread factor is unverified - confirm it against a licensed copy of the code you are working to before relying on that column.

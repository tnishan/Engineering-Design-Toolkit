7. Notes and warnings

Microllam LVL 2.0E design values assume lateral support at bearings and at 24 in. (610 mm) on-centre maximum along the span, per the TJ-9500 general assumptions.
Multi-ply SCL must be fastened per TJ-9500 pages 16-18. Side-loaded plies beyond two require through-bolting or top-loading; confirm the connection detail transfers load to every ply.
Total deflection excludes creep (creep factor 1.0). CSA O86-19 Cl. 4.5.3 requires long-term deflection under permanent load to be multiplied by 2.0 for sawn lumber in dry service.
